TRAVEL NOTESOCT 18, 2026

The joy of taking the scenic route

Sometimes the best part of a journey is everything you notice along the way. Leave a little room for unexpected turns.

Explore this story

There is something special about choosing the slower road. On a trip, it might mean taking the coastal path instead of the motorway, stopping at a small café, or wandering through a street you had not planned to visit.

When we leave a little space in our plans, we notice more: the changing light, local shop windows, the sound of the sea and conversations that turn into memories. The destination still matters, but the moments in between often become the stories we tell later.

Try this: On your next day out, leave one hour unplanned. Follow a view, a recommendation or simply your curiosity.

CREATIVE LIVINGOCT 12, 2026

Make something just because

Try a new colour, fill a page or make a tiny handmade gift. Creativity can be about enjoying the process.

Explore this story

Creativity does not have to become a side hustle or a finished masterpiece. Sometimes it is enough to fill a page with colour, rearrange a corner of your room, take photographs on a walk or make a card for someone you love.

Making things without judging the result gives your mind room to play. Start small, use what you already have and let the process be the reward. A few imperfect minutes of making can feel surprisingly refreshing.

Try this: Set a ten-minute timer and create something with no plan to share it. Keep it just for yourself.

EVERYDAY INSPIRATIONOCT 06, 2026

Find beauty in the little things

A favourite mug, a quiet morning or sunlight on the wall. Small moments deserve our attention too.

Explore this story

Beauty is not always found in big celebrations or faraway places. It can be the first sip of tea, a familiar song, clean sheets or a patch of afternoon sun moving slowly across the floor.

Noticing these details does not mean every day has to be perfect. It simply gives ordinary life a little more attention. A small pause can help us feel present, even on a busy day.

Try this: Before bed, write down three small things that made today feel good. They do not need to be important to anyone else.

SLOW LIVINGSEP 29, 2026

A softer way to spend your Sunday

Make space for a slower start: a simple breakfast, a favourite book and time away from the rush.

Explore this story

A softer Sunday does not need an elaborate routine. Open the curtains, make a breakfast you enjoy and give yourself time to wake up before reaching for your to-do list.

You might read a few pages, step outside for fresh air, call someone you miss or prepare one simple thing for the week ahead. The point is not to do nothing; it is to choose a gentler pace and make room for what restores you.

Try this: Pick one small ritual that makes Sunday feel like yours, and protect a little time for it.
